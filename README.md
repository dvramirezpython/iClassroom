# iClassroom
Research project about estimating students' engagement, attention, and interaction in a classroom. 

**Publishes scientific works**

> Valdes-Ramirez, D. et al. Estimating Students’ Attention in a Classroom from Behavioral Observation with Computer Vision Techniques. In Hosseini, S., Beitzel, B., Köhler, M. & Sanjakdar, F. (eds.) Technological Advancements and Innovations in Future of Education, FOE, 61–71, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-97-4931-7_7 (Transactions on Computer Systems and Networks, 2023).
>
> Valdes-Ramirez, D., Sobrino Caamal, V., Huertas Cardozo, J. I. & Zavala, G. Edge computing model for real-time classroom behavior analysis in face-to-face lessons. In 22nd International Conference Distributed Computing and Artificial Intelligence, vol. Special Sessions II, DOI: http://hdl.handle.net/10366/167172 (Springer, 2025).
>
> Valdes-Ramirez, D. et al. A deep learning approach to estimating interaction levels in face-to-face lessons. Comput. Educ. Artif. Intell. 10, 100528, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2025.100528 (2026).
